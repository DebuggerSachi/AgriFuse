Why CarbonAid?

For Farmers

Monetize your sustainable practices. Turn every tree you plant into a financial asset through carbon credits.

For Industries

Meet your CSR goals and regulatory requirements by purchasing verified carbon credits from local farmers.

For the Planet

Together, we can reduce the global carbon footprint and restore our ecosystem for future generations.

How It Works

1. Plant & Log

Farmers plant trees and log their activities on our platform with proof of growth.

2. Earn Credits

Our platform automatically calculates CO2 absorption and issues carbon credits.

3. Exchange

Industries buy these credits on our marketplace, providing direct income to farmers.
